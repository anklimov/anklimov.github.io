Detailed Description

1-Wire temperature sensing for thermostats.

Reads DS18S20/DS18B20 digital thermometers (device type 0x28) through a DS2482-100 I2C-to-1-Wire bridge (or a software 1-Wire driver on a dedicated pin). Each configured sensor is polled periodically and its temperature is published to the item's /val subtopic.
