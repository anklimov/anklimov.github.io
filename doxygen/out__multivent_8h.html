#define AC_BOOST_HIGH_TEMP   30

Boost parameters of the shared AC node.

When the balance of the zone PID outputs exceeds AC_BOOST_TRESHOLD (230), the AC setpoint is boosted to AC_BOOST_HIGH_TEMP (30) for heating or AC_BOOST_LOW_TEMP (17) for cooling, and restored to AC_PRESET_TEMP (21) when the balance falls back within AC_BOOST_DEADBAND (10). AC_HEATCOOL_DEADBAND is the temperature deadband of HEATCOOL zones.
